References

Every verse and hadith used on the site, with full references and links to the original sources

Method and sources

Source rule: all the material on this site is taken from only three sources: the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. No other hadith collection, tafsir or scholar's statement is quoted. The explanatory English text (mirroring the Urdu) serves only to connect the texts to one another.

The Quran: the Arabic text is in the Uthmani script (quran-uthmani). The English translation is Saheeh International (edition en.sahih); the Urdu version uses the translation of Maulana Muhammad Junagarhi (ur.junagarhi). All were fetched from api.alquran.cloud, and the surah and verse number of every verse were checked. References are given as "Surah name, surah:verse" and each one links to quran.com.

Hadith: the Arabic text of each hadith (with its isnad) is taken from the corresponding page on sunnah.com. The English translation is the one published on that same sunnah.com page (the same number as the Arabic). The Urdu version's translation comes from the urd-bukhari / urd-muslim editions of fawazahmed0/hadith-api, checked against the content of each hadith. English hadith translations are reproduced as published, including their original spelling.

Urdu version (اردو): the site is also available in Urdu (click «اردو» at the top). The Arabic text is identical in both versions.

Hadith numbering: all numbers follow the sunnah.com "Reference" numbering (Sahih al-Bukhari: the common standard numbering; Sahih Muslim: the sunnah.com reference number, which generally matches the numbering of Muhammad Fu'ad 'Abd al-Baqi). Where sunnah.com lists several narrations under one number (e.g. 8a, 35b, 2564c, 2653b), the link points to that specific narration and the number is annotated.

Note: in the "Book of Destiny" (Kitab al-Qadar) of Sahih Muslim, the sunnah.com numbering in places differs by one from some other printed editions. On this site the hadith "everything is by decree, even incapacity and ability" is listed as 2656 following sunnah.com; in some editions the same hadith is number 2655. Likewise, the hadith about the decrees being written fifty thousand years beforehand is 2653b on sunnah.com.

Totals: altogether 96 verses and 55 hadith are used on this site (lessons, diagrams and quizzes).
