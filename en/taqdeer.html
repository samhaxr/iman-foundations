Quiz for this lesson

In the Hadith of Jibril the definition of faith ends: «وَتُؤْمِنَ بِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ»: and that you believe in the decree, its good and its bad (Sahih Muslim 8). At the start of this same hadith, 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both) declared himself free of those who deny the decree. In this lesson we will see which aspects of the decree the Quran and the two Sahihs describe, and what effect this belief should have on our actions and on how we face hardship.

1. Decree is a pillar of faith

In the Hadith of Jibril six pillars of faith are stated, and the last pillar is belief in the decree, its good and its bad. Ibn 'Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both) said that even if a denier of the decree spent gold equal to Mount Uhud, Allah would not accept it from him until he believes in the decree.

Hadith of Jibril (narrated by 'Umar)

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 8

«أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاللَّهِ وَمَلاَئِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ وَتُؤْمِنَ بِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهِ … أَنْ تَعْبُدَ اللَّهَ كَأَنَّكَ تَرَاهُ فَإِنْ لَمْ تَكُنْ تَرَاهُ فَإِنَّهُ يَرَاكَ»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

It is narrated on the authority of Yahya b. Ya'mur that the first man who discussed qadr (Divine Decree) in Basra was Ma'bad al-Juhani. I along with Humaid b. 'Abdur-Rahman Himyari set out for pilgrimage or for 'Umrah and said: Should it so happen that we come into contact with one of the Companions of the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) we shall ask him about what is talked about taqdir (Divine Decree). Accidentally we came across Abdullah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, while he was entering the mosque. My companion and I surrounded him. One of us (stood) on his right and the other stood on his left. I expected that my companion would authorize me to speak. I therefore said: Abu Abdur Rahman! There have appeared some people in our land who recite the Qur'an and pursue knowledge. And then after talking about their affairs, added: They (such people) claim that there is no such thing as Divine Decree and events are not predestined. He (Abdullah ibn Umar) said: When you happen to meet such people tell them that I have nothing to do with them and they have nothing to do with me. And verily they are in no way responsible for my (belief). Abdullah ibn Umar swore by Him (the Lord) (and said): If any one of them (who does not believe in the Divine Decree) had with him gold equal to the bulk of (the mountain) Uhud and spent it (in the way of Allah), Allah would not accept it unless he affirmed his faith in Divine Decree. He further said: My father, Umar ibn al-Khattab, told me: One day we were sitting in the company of Allah's Apostle (peace be upon him) when there appeared before us a man dressed in pure white clothes, his hair extraordinarily black. There were no signs of travel on him. None amongst us recognized him. At last he sat with the Apostle (peace be upon him) He knelt before him placed his palms on his thighs and said: Muhammad, inform me about al-Islam. The Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) said: Al-Islam implies that you testify that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the messenger of Allah, and you establish prayer, pay Zakat, observe the fast of Ramadan, and perform pilgrimage to the (House) if you are solvent enough (to bear the expense of) the journey. He (the inquirer) said: You have told the truth. He (Umar ibn al-Khattab) said: It amazed us that he would put the question and then he would himself verify the truth. He (the inquirer) said: Inform me about Iman (faith). He (the Holy Prophet) replied: That you affirm your faith in Allah, in His angels, in His Books, in His Apostles, in the Day of Judgment, and you affirm your faith in the Divine Decree about good and evil. He (the inquirer) said: You have told the truth. He (the inquirer) again said: Inform me about al-Ihsan (performance of good deeds). He (the Holy Prophet) said: That you worship Allah as if you are seeing Him, for though you don't see Him, He, verily, sees you. He (the enquirer) again said: Inform me about the hour (of the Doom). He (the Holy Prophet) remarked: One who is asked knows no more than the one who is inquiring (about it). He (the inquirer) said: Tell me some of its indications. He (the Holy Prophet) said: That the slave-girl will give birth to her mistress and master, that you will find barefooted, destitute goat-herds vying with one another in the construction of magnificent buildings. He (the narrator, Umar ibn al-Khattab) said: Then he (the inquirer) went on his way but I stayed with him (the Holy Prophet) for a long while. He then, said to me: Umar, do you know who this inquirer was? I replied: Allah and His Apostle knows best. He (the Holy Prophet) remarked: He was Gabriel (the angel). He came to you in order to instruct you in matters of religion.

2. Everything is by measure

«إِنَّا كُلَّ شَىْءٍ خَلَقْنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٍ»: We created everything with measure. Allah created everything and then determined it with precise measure. Allah's command is a decree determined, and Allah has set a measure for everything. The Prophet ﷺ said: everything is by decree, even incapacity and ability.

The QuranSurah Al-Qamar, 54:49

إِنَّا كُلَّ شَىْءٍ خَلَقْنَٰهُ بِقَدَرٍۢ ﴿٤٩﴾

Indeed, all things We created with predestination. (49)

Translation: Saheeh International

3. First aspect: Allah's knowledge

The keys of the unseen are with Allah alone; everything on land and in the sea, and every leaf that falls, is within His knowledge. The provision of every creature that moves on the earth is upon Allah, and He knows its place of dwelling and place of storage. Everything in the heaven and the earth is within Allah's knowledge.

The QuranSurah Al-An'aam, 6:59

۞ وَعِندَهُۥ مَفَاتِحُ ٱلْغَيْبِ لَا يَعْلَمُهَآ إِلَّا هُوَ ۚ وَيَعْلَمُ مَا فِى ٱلْبَرِّ وَٱلْبَحْرِ ۚ وَمَا تَسْقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعْلَمُهَا وَلَا حَبَّةٍۢ فِى ظُلُمَٰتِ ٱلْأَرْضِ وَلَا رَطْبٍۢ وَلَا يَابِسٍ إِلَّا فِى كِتَٰبٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ ﴿٥٩﴾

And with Him are the keys of the unseen; none knows them except Him. And He knows what is on the land and in the sea. Not a leaf falls but that He knows it. And no grain is there within the darknesses of the earth and no moist or dry [thing] but that it is [written] in a clear record. (59)

And there is no creature on earth but that upon Allah is its provision, and He knows its place of dwelling and place of storage. All is in a clear register. (6)

4. Second aspect: being written (kitabah)

The same verse of Surat al-Hajj adds to knowledge that all of this is (written) in a Book. No disaster strikes upon the earth or among yourselves except that it is in a Book before We bring it into being. Allah records every deed and every trace, and has preserved everything in a clear register. The Prophet ﷺ said: Allah wrote the decrees of the creatures fifty thousand years before He created the heavens and the earth. And he said: Allah existed and nothing existed besides Him; His Throne was upon the water, and He wrote everything in the Dhikr (the Preserved Tablet). In the mother's womb too, the angel writes four things: deeds, provision, life-span, and whether wretched or blessed.

The QuranSurah Al-Hadid, 57:22-23

5. Third aspect: Allah's will (mashi'ah)

«وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ»: and you do not will except that Allah wills, Lord of the worlds. The same is stated in Surat ad-Dahr, together with the fact that Allah is All-Knowing and Wise. These verses affirm "willing" for the servant too, and also that it is subject to Allah's will. The Prophet ﷺ taught these words to say at a time of hardship: «قَدَرُ اللَّهِ وَمَا شَاءَ فَعَلَ»: it is Allah's decree, and He did what He willed.

7. Decree and action: "Do deeds; for each it is made easy"

When the Prophet ﷺ told them that each person's place in Paradise or Hell has already been written, the Companions asked: shall we not then rely on that and give up deeds? He ﷺ said: do deeds, for each person is facilitated for what he was created for; then he recited the verses of Surat al-Layl: as for he who gives and fears Allah and believes in the best reward, We will ease him toward ease. That is, belief in decree is not an excuse to abandon deeds, but an encouragement to act.

8. The good and bad of decree: the believer's attitude to hardship

No disaster strikes except by Allah's permission, and whoever believes in Allah, He guides his heart. Nothing will befall us except what Allah has written for us; He is our Protector, and upon Allah let the believers rely. The wisdom given for hardships being written beforehand is that you not grieve over what has escaped you nor exult over what He has given you. Every affair of the believer is good: when blessed he is grateful, and when harmed he is patient. Be a strong believer, be keen on what benefits you, seek Allah's help, and when a loss comes do not say "if only", for that opens the door to the work of Satan. And the Prophet ﷺ commanded seeking refuge with Allah from «سُوءِ الْقَضَاءِ» (an evil judgement / evil decree).

The QuranSurah At-Taghaabun, 64:11

مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ ۗ وَمَن يُؤْمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ يَهْدِ قَلْبَهُۥ ۚ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍ عَلِيمٌۭ ﴿١١﴾

No disaster strikes except by permission of Allah. And whoever believes in Allah - He will guide his heart. And Allah is Knowing of all things. (11)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah At-Tawba, 9:51

قُل لَّن يُصِيبَنَآ إِلَّا مَا كَتَبَ ٱللَّهُ لَنَا هُوَ مَوْلَىٰنَا ۚ وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ﴿٥١﴾

Say, "Never will we be struck except by what Allah has decreed for us; He is our protector." And upon Allah let the believers rely. (51)

Translation: Saheeh International

The affair of the believer is amazing: gratitude and patience

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 2999

«عَجَبًا لأَمْرِ الْمُؤْمِنِ إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ … إِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ سَرَّاءُ شَكَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ ضَرَّاءُ صَبَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Suhaib reported that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said: Strange are the ways of a believer for there is good in every affair of his and this is not the case with anyone else except in the case of a believer for if he has an occasion to feel delight, he thanks (God), thus there is a good for him in it, and if he gets into trouble and shows resignation (and endures it patiently), there is a good for him in it.

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا هَدَّابُ بْنُ خَالِدٍ الأَزْدِيُّ ، وَشَيْبَانُ بْنُ فَرُّوخَ ، جَمِيعًا عَنْ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ ، - وَاللَّفْظُ لِشَيْبَانَ - حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا ثَابِتٌ ، عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبِي لَيْلَى ، عَنْ صُهَيْبٍ ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ عَجَبًا لأَمْرِ الْمُؤْمِنِ إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ وَلَيْسَ ذَاكَ لأَحَدٍ إِلاَّ لِلْمُؤْمِنِ إِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ سَرَّاءُ شَكَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ ضَرَّاءُ صَبَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ ‏" ‏ ‏.‏

Seeking refuge from an evil decree (su' al-qada')

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6616

«تَعَوَّذُوا بِاللَّهِ مِنْ جَهْدِ الْبَلاَءِ، وَدَرَكِ الشَّقَاءِ، وَسُوءِ الْقَضَاءِ، وَشَمَاتَةِ الأَعْدَاءِ»

Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Take refuge with Allah from the difficulties of severe calamities, from having an evil end and a bad fate and from the malicious joy of your enemies."

9. The argument between Adam and Musa (peace be upon them)

The Prophet ﷺ related that Musa (peace be upon him) said to Adam (peace be upon him): you deprived us and expelled us from Paradise. Adam (peace be upon him) said: do you blame me for something that Allah had written in my decree forty years before He created me? The Prophet ﷺ said three times that Adam got the better of Musa in the argument.

The argument between Adam and Musa (peace be upon them)

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6614

«أَتَلُومُنِي عَلَى أَمْرٍ قَدَّرَ اللَّهُ عَلَىَّ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَخْلُقَنِي بِأَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Adam and Moses argued with each other. Moses said to Adam. 'O Adam! You are our father who disappointed us and turned us out of Paradise.' Then Adam said to him, 'O Moses! Allah favored you with His talk (talked to you directly) and He wrote (the Torah) for you with His Own Hand. Do you blame me for action which Allah had written in my fate forty years before my creation?' So Adam confuted Moses, Adam confuted Moses," the Prophet (ﷺ) added, repeating the Statement three times.

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ ، قَالَ حَفِظْنَاهُ مِنْ عَمْرٍو عَنْ طَاوُسٍ ، سَمِعْتُ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ احْتَجَّ آدَمُ وَمُوسَى، فَقَالَ لَهُ مُوسَى يَا آدَمُ أَنْتَ أَبُونَا خَيَّبْتَنَا وَأَخْرَجْتَنَا مِنَ الْجَنَّةِ‏.‏ قَالَ لَهُ آدَمُ يَا مُوسَى اصْطَفَاكَ اللَّهُ بِكَلاَمِهِ، وَخَطَّ لَكَ بِيَدِهِ، أَتَلُومُنِي عَلَى أَمْرٍ قَدَّرَ اللَّهُ عَلَىَّ قَبْلَ أَنْ يَخْلُقَنِي بِأَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً‏.‏ فَحَجَّ آدَمُ مُوسَى، فَحَجَّ آدَمُ مُوسَى ‏"‏ثَلاَثًا‏.‏ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الزِّنَادِ ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَهُ ‏.‏

Summary

  • Belief in decree is the sixth pillar of faith (Sahih Muslim 8).
  • Knowledge: the keys of the unseen are with Allah (al-An'am 6:59).
  • Writing: written fifty thousand years before the heavens and earth (Sahih Muslim 2653; al-Hadid 57:22).
  • Will: you do not will except what Allah wills (at-Takwir 81:29).
  • Creation: Allah created you and what you do (as-Saffat 37:96).
  • Decree does not stop action: "Do deeds; for each it is made easy" (Sahih al-Bukhari 4949).
  • Patience in hardship, gratitude in blessing, and instead of "if only", «Qadar Allah wa ma sha'a fa'al» (Sahih Muslim 2999, 2664).
