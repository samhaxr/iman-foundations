The most fundamental question before a human being is: why was I created? Allah did not leave this question to human speculation; He stated it plainly in His Book. In this lesson we will read, in order, only texts from the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim; the explanation in between serves only to connect those texts to one another.

1. The purpose of creation: worship of Allah alone

In these verses of Surat adh-Dhariyat the purpose is stated in a single sentence: «وَمَا خَلَقْتُ ٱلْجِنَّ وَٱلْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ», that is, jinn and mankind were created only so that they would worship Allah. In the same place Allah also made clear that He wants no provision from His servants, nor that they feed Him; He Himself is the Provider, the Possessor of strength, the Firm.

The QuranSurah Adh-Dhaariyat, 51:56-58

وَمَا خَلَقْتُ ٱلْجِنَّ وَٱلْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ ﴿٥٦﴾ مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنْهُم مِّن رِّزْقٍۢ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطْعِمُونِ ﴿٥٧﴾ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلْقُوَّةِ ٱلْمَتِينُ ﴿٥٨﴾

And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me. (56) I do not want from them any provision, nor do I want them to feed Me. (57) Indeed, it is Allah who is the [continual] Provider, the firm possessor of strength. (58)

Translation: Saheeh International

2. Allah's right over His servants

The Prophet ﷺ, with Mu'adh ibn Jabal riding behind him, taught this very point through question and answer: Allah's right over His servants is that they worship Him and do not associate anything with Him; and the servants' right over Allah is that He does not punish whoever does not associate partners with Him. This hadith is the practical explanation of the verse on the purpose of creation: worship must be present, and it must be free of shirk.

Allah's right over His servants, and the servants' right over Allah

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 2856

«فَإِنَّ حَقَّ اللَّهِ عَلَى الْعِبَادِ أَنْ يَعْبُدُوهُ وَلاَ يُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَحَقَّ الْعِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ أَنْ لاَ يُعَذِّبَ مَنْ لاَ يُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated Mu`adh: I was a companion rider of the Prophet (ﷺ) on a donkey called 'Ufair. The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, "O Mu`adh! Do you know what Allah's right on His slaves is, and what the right of His slaves on Him is?" I replied, "Allah and His Apostle know better." He said, "Allah's right on His slaves is that they should worship Him (Alone) and should not worship any besides Him. And slave's right on Allah is that He should not punish him who worships none besides Him." I said, "O Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Should I not inform the people of this good news?" He said, "Do not inform them of it, lest they should depend on it (absolutely).

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ ، سَمِعَ يَحْيَى بْنَ آدَمَ ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو الأَحْوَصِ ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ ، عَنْ عَمْرِو بْنِ مَيْمُونٍ ، عَنْ مُعَاذٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنْتُ رِدْفَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَلَى حِمَارٍ يُقَالُ لَهُ عُفَيْرٌ، فَقَالَ ‏"‏ يَا مُعَاذُ، هَلْ تَدْرِي حَقَّ اللَّهِ عَلَى عِبَادِهِ وَمَا حَقُّ الْعِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏"‏ فَإِنَّ حَقَّ اللَّهِ عَلَى الْعِبَادِ أَنْ يَعْبُدُوهُ وَلاَ يُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَحَقَّ الْعِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ أَنْ لاَ يُعَذِّبَ مَنْ لاَ يُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا ‏"‏‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، أَفَلاَ أُبَشِّرُ بِهِ النَّاسَ قَالَ ‏"‏ لاَ تُبَشِّرْهُمْ فَيَتَّكِلُوا ‏" ‏‏.‏

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 30

«يَعْبُدُوهُ وَلاَ يُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا … هَلْ تَدْرِي مَا حَقُّ الْعِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ إِذَا فَعَلُوا ذَلِكَ»

Jabal: To which I replied: At your beck and call, and at your pleasure, Messenger of Allah! He moved along for a few minutes, when again he said: Mu'adh b. Jabal: To which I replied: At your beck and call, and at your pleasure, Messenger of Allah! He then again moved along for a few minutes and said: Mu'adh b. Jabal: To which I replied. At your beck and call, and at your pleasure. Messenger of Allah He, (the Holy Prophet) said: Do you know what right has Allah upon His servants? I said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He (the Holy Prophet) said: Verily the right of Allah over His servants is that they should worship Him, not associating anything with Him. He (the Holy Prophet) with Mu'adh behind him, moved along for a few minutes and said: Mu'adh b. Jabal: To which I replied: At your beck and call, and at your pleasure, Messenger of Allah! Jabal) replied: Allah and His Messenger know best. (Upon this) he (the Holy Prophet) remarked: That He would not torment them (with the fire of Hell).

3. The single call of every messenger

To remind people of the purpose of their creation, Allah sent a messenger to every nation, and all of them carried one message: worship Allah alone and avoid taghut (false objects of worship besides Allah). In Surat al-Anbiya' He said that to every messenger it was revealed that there is no god but Me, so worship Me. In Surat al-Baqarah all mankind is addressed: worship your Lord who created you and those before you.

The QuranSurah An-Nahl, 16:36

وَلَقَدْ بَعَثْنَا فِى كُلِّ أُمَّةٍۢ رَّسُولًا أَنِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱجْتَنِبُوا۟ ٱلطَّٰغُوتَ ۖ فَمِنْهُم مَّنْ هَدَى ٱللَّهُ وَمِنْهُم مَّنْ حَقَّتْ عَلَيْهِ ٱلضَّلَٰلَةُ ۚ فَسِيرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ فَٱنظُرُوا۟ كَيْفَ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ ٱلْمُكَذِّبِينَ ﴿٣٦﴾

And We certainly sent into every nation a messenger, [saying], "Worship Allah and avoid Taghut." And among them were those whom Allah guided, and among them were those upon whom error was [deservedly] decreed. So proceed through the earth and observe how was the end of the deniers. (36)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Anbiyaa, 21:25

وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَا مِن قَبْلِكَ مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا نُوحِىٓ إِلَيْهِ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعْبُدُونِ ﴿٢٥﴾

And We sent not before you any messenger except that We revealed to him that, "There is no deity except Me, so worship Me." (25)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Baqara, 2:21

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُمْ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ ﴿٢١﴾

O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, that you may become righteous - (21)

Translation: Saheeh International

4. Worship must be sincere and must encompass the whole of life

Surat al-Bayyinah tells us that people were commanded only to worship Allah, making the religion sincerely His. In Surat al-An'am the Prophet ﷺ was commanded to declare that my prayer, my worship, my living and my dying are all for Allah, Lord of the worlds, who has no partner. So worship is not the name of a few rituals only; the whole of life is to be for Allah. In Surat al-Fatihah every person who prays repeats this pledge daily: You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. And the acceptance of a deed depends on the intention, as in the first hadith of Sahih al-Bukhari.

The QuranSurah Al-Bayyina, 98:5

وَمَآ أُمِرُوٓا۟ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ حُنَفَآءَ وَيُقِيمُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَيُؤْتُوا۟ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ ٱلْقَيِّمَةِ ﴿٥﴾

And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion. (5)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-An'aam, 6:162-163

قُلْ إِنَّ صَلَاتِى وَنُسُكِى وَمَحْيَاىَ وَمَمَاتِى لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ﴿١٦٢﴾ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُۥ ۖ وَبِذَٰلِكَ أُمِرْتُ وَأَنَا۠ أَوَّلُ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ ﴿١٦٣﴾

Say, "Indeed, my prayer, my rites of sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds. (162) No partner has He. And this I have been commanded, and I am the first [among you] of the Muslims." (163)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Faatiha, 1:5

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ﴿٥﴾

It is You we worship and You we ask for help. (5)

Translation: Saheeh International

Deeds are judged by intentions

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 1

«إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated 'Umar bin Al-Khattab: I heard Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) saying, "The reward of deeds depends upon the intentions and every person will get the reward according to what he has intended. So whoever emigrated for worldly benefits or for a woman to marry, his emigration was for what he emigrated for."

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا الْحُمَيْدِيُّ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ الزُّبَيْرِ ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ الأَنْصَارِيُّ ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ التَّيْمِيُّ ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ عَلْقَمَةَ بْنَ وَقَّاصٍ اللَّيْثِيَّ ، يَقُولُ سَمِعْتُ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ عَلَى الْمِنْبَرِ قَالَ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏"‏ إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى، فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى دُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوْ إِلَى امْرَأَةٍ يَنْكِحُهَا فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِ ‏" ‏‏.‏

5. Life and death: for a test

Alongside the purpose of worship, the Quran also tells us that death and life were created so that Allah may test who is best in deeds. The adornment of the earth is part of this same test, and man was made hearing and seeing precisely so that he could be tested. In other words, worship is the purpose and the life of this world is its examination hall.

The QuranSurah Al-Mulk, 67:2

ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْمَوْتَ وَٱلْحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًۭا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفُورُ ﴿٢﴾

[He] who created death and life to test you [as to] which of you is best in deed - and He is the Exalted in Might, the Forgiving - (2)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Kahf, 18:7

إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا مَا عَلَى ٱلْأَرْضِ زِينَةًۭ لَّهَا لِنَبْلُوَهُمْ أَيُّهُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًۭا ﴿٧﴾

Indeed, We have made that which is on the earth adornment for it that We may test them [as to] which of them is best in deed. (7)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Insaan, 76:2

إِنَّا خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطْفَةٍ أَمْشَاجٍۢ نَّبْتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلْنَٰهُ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًا ﴿٢﴾

Indeed, We created man from a sperm-drop mixture that We may try him; and We made him hearing and seeing. (2)

Translation: Saheeh International

6. Man has not been left without purpose

Allah warns in the form of a question: did you think that We created you without purpose and that you would not be returned to Us? Then He said that Allah is the true King; there is no god but Him. Surat al-Qiyamah asks the same question: does man think he will be left neglected? That is, accountability is tied to the purpose.

The QuranSurah Al-Muminoon, 23:115-116

أَفَحَسِبْتُمْ أَنَّمَا خَلَقْنَٰكُمْ عَبَثًۭا وَأَنَّكُمْ إِلَيْنَا لَا تُرْجَعُونَ ﴿١١٥﴾ فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْحَقُّ ۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْكَرِيمِ ﴿١١٦﴾

Then did you think that We created you uselessly and that to Us you would not be returned?" (115) So exalted is Allah, the Sovereign, the Truth; there is no deity except Him, Lord of the Noble Throne. (116)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Qiyaama, 75:36

أَيَحْسَبُ ٱلْإِنسَٰنُ أَن يُتْرَكَ سُدًى ﴿٣٦﴾

Does man think that he will be left neglected? (36)

Translation: Saheeh International

7. The fitrah and the primordial covenant

Allah took from the children of Adam, drawing them from their loins, an acknowledgement: «أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ»: Am I not your Lord? They all said: Yes indeed! And in Surat ar-Rum the command is to hold firm to the fitrah of Allah upon which He created people. The Prophet ﷺ said that every child is born upon the fitrah, then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian.

The QuranSurah Al-A'raaf, 7:172

وَإِذْ أَخَذَ رَبُّكَ مِنۢ بَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ مِن ظُهُورِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمْ وَأَشْهَدَهُمْ عَلَىٰٓ أَنفُسِهِمْ أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ ۖ قَالُوا۟ بَلَىٰ ۛ شَهِدْنَآ ۛ أَن تَقُولُوا۟ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَٰمَةِ إِنَّا كُنَّا عَنْ هَٰذَا غَٰفِلِينَ ﴿١٧٢﴾

And [mention] when your Lord took from the children of Adam - from their loins - their descendants and made them testify of themselves, [saying to them], "Am I not your Lord?" 8. Worship until the last breath, and valuing time

Allah commanded His Prophet ﷺ: keep worshipping your Lord until death comes to you. In a hadith qudsi it is stated that the servant gains the nearness to Allah that He loves most through the obligatory deeds, then keeps drawing nearer through voluntary deeds until Allah loves him. The example of one who remembers his Lord and one who does not is like the living and the dead. And health and free time are two blessings that most people do not value, even though they are the very capital for worship.

The QuranSurah Al-Hijr, 15:99

وَٱعْبُدْ رَبَّكَ حَتَّىٰ يَأْتِيَكَ ٱلْيَقِينُ ﴿٩٩﴾

And worship your Lord until there comes to you the certainty (death). (99)

And the most beloved things with which My slave comes nearer to Me, is what I have enjoined upon him; and My slave keeps on coming closer to Me through performing Nawafil (praying or doing extra deeds besides what is obligatory) till I love him, so I become his sense of hearing with which he hears, and his sense of sight with which he sees, and his hand with which he grips, and his leg with which he walks; and if he asks Me, I will give him, and if he asks My protection (Refuge), I will protect him; (i.e. give him My Refuge) and I do not hesitate to do anything as I hesitate to take the soul of the believer, for he hates death, and I hate to disappoint him."

9. Allah is free of need; it is we who are in need

In the hadith qudsi "O My servants", Allah says that you can neither harm Me nor benefit Me; if all of you, the first and the last, mankind and jinn, were as the most pious heart among you, that would add nothing to His dominion, and if they were all as the most wicked, it would take nothing away. This accords with verses 57-58 of Surat adh-Dhariyat: the command to worship is not for any need of Allah; guidance, food, clothing and forgiveness: all of it we ask from Him.

Hadith Qudsi: "O My servants!"

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 2577

«يَا عِبَادِي إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا فَلاَ تَظَالَمُوا … مَا زَادَ ذَلِكَ فِي مُلْكِي شَيْئًا»

O My servants, all of you are astray except for those I have guided, so seek guidance of Me and I shall guide you, O My servants, all of you are hungry except for those I have fed, so seek food of Me and I shall feed you. O My servants, all of you are naked except for those I have clothed, so seek clothing of Me and I shall clothe you. O My servants, you sin by night and by day, and I forgive all sins, so seek forgiveness of Me and I shall forgive you. O My servants, you will not attain harming Me so as to harm Me, and will not attain benefitting Me so as to benefit Me. O My servants, were the first of you and the last of you, the human of you and the jinn of you to be as pious as the most pious heart of any one man of you, that would not increase My dominion in anything. O My servants, were the first of you and the last of you, the human of you and the jinn of you to be as wicked as the most wicked heart of any one man of you, that would not decrease My dominion in anything. O My servants, were the first of you and the last of you, the human of you and the jinn of you to rise up in one place and make a request of Me, and were I to give everyone what he requested, that would not decrease what I have, any more that a needle decreases the sea if put into it. O My servants, it is but your deeds that I record for you and then recompense you for. So let him who finds good, praise Allah, and let him who finds other than that blame no one but himself." 10. The end of turning away from the purpose

On the Day of Resurrection, Allah will ask the person in Hell with the lightest punishment: if you had everything on earth, would you give it as ransom? He will say: Yes. Allah will say: I asked of you something far easier than this when you were in the loins of Adam (that you not associate anything with Me), but you refused everything except shirk.

«قَدْ أَرَدْتُ مِنْكَ أَهْوَنَ مِنْ هَذَا وَأَنْتَ فِي صُلْبِ آدَمَ أَنْ لاَ تُشْرِكَ»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Anas b. Malik reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying: Allah, the Exalted and High, would say to one who shall have to undergo the least torture (on the Day of Resurrection): Would you like to go as ransom if you had all worldly riches; he would say: Yes. Allah would say to him: When you were in the loins of Adam, I demanded from you something easier than this that you should not associate anything with Me. (The narrator says): I think He also said: I would not cause you to enter Hell-Fire but you defied and attributed Divinity (to others besides Me).

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُعَاذٍ الْعَنْبَرِيُّ ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ ، عَنْ أَبِي عِمْرَانَ الْجَوْنِيِّ ، عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ يَقُولُ اللَّهُ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى لأَهْوَنِ أَهْلِ النَّارِ عَذَابًا لَوْ كَانَتْ لَكَ الدُّنْيَا وَمَا فِيهَا أَكُنْتَ مُفْتَدِيًا بِهَا فَيَقُولُ نَعَمْ فَيَقُولُ قَدْ أَرَدْتُ مِنْكَ أَهْوَنَ مِنْ هَذَا وَأَنْتَ فِي صُلْبِ آدَمَ أَنْ لاَ تُشْرِكَ - أَحْسَبُهُ قَالَ - وَلاَ أُدْخِلَكَ النَّارَ فَأَبَيْتَ إِلاَّ الشِّرْكَ ‏" ‏ ‏.‏

Summary

  • The purpose of creating jinn and mankind is the worship of Allah alone (adh-Dhariyat 51:56).
  • Allah has no need of His servants; He alone is the Provider (adh-Dhariyat 51:57-58; Sahih Muslim 2577).
  • Allah's right over His servants: to worship Him and avoid shirk (Sahih al-Bukhari 2856; Sahih Muslim 30).
  • This was the call of every messenger (an-Nahl 16:36; al-Anbiya' 21:25).
  • Worship must be sincere and encompass all of life (al-Bayyinah 98:5; al-An'am 6:162-163).
  • Life is a test and man is accountable (al-Mulk 67:2; al-Mu'minun 23:115).
