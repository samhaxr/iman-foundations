«لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ» is the first pillar of the foundation of Islam, the highest branch of faith, and the summary of the call of all the messengers. In this lesson we will see what the Quran and the two Sahihs say this kalimah means, how negation and affirmation are combined in it, and what the texts require of the one who says it.

The kalimah of tawhid: negation and affirmation

1. The meaning of the kalimah: there is no god (worthy of worship) except Allah

The Quran mentions this kalimah word for word and commands knowledge of it: «فَٱعْلَمْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ»: so know that there is no god except Allah. And Allah Himself, the angels and those of knowledge bore witness to it. In the Quran this kalimah often comes joined to the command to worship, as in «لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعْبُدُونِ»: there is no god except Me, so worship Me (al-Anbiya' 21:25; see also Ta-Ha 20:14). From this it is clear that "god" (ilah) here means the one who is worshipped.

The QuranSurah Muhammad, 47:19

فَٱعْلَمْ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱسْتَغْفِرْ لِذَنۢبِكَ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَٱلْمُؤْمِنَٰتِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مُتَقَلَّبَكُمْ وَمَثْوَىٰكُمْ ﴿١٩﴾

So know, [O Muhammad], that there is no deity except Allah and ask forgiveness for your sin and for the believing men and believing women. And Allah knows of your movement and your resting place. (19)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Aal-i-Imraan, 3:18

شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلْمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْعِلْمِ قَآئِمًۢا بِٱلْقِسْطِ ۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ ﴿١٨﴾

Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and [so do] the angels and those of knowledge - [that He is] maintaining [creation] in justice. There is no deity except Him, the Exalted in Might, the Wise. (18)

Translation: Saheeh International

2. Two pillars: negation (لَا إِلٰهَ) and affirmation (إِلَّا اللّٰهُ)

The first part of the kalimah, «لَا إِلٰهَ», negates every object of worship, and the second part, «إِلَّا اللّٰهُ», affirms worship for Allah alone. The Quran combined both pillars in one verse: «فَمَن يَكْفُرْ بِٱلطَّٰغُوتِ وَيُؤْمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱسْتَمْسَكَ بِٱلْعُرْوَةِ ٱلْوُثْقَىٰ»: whoever disbelieves in taghut (negation) and believes in Allah (affirmation) has grasped the firmest handhold. The hadith in Sahih Muslim also puts both together: whoever says La ilaha illallah and rejects whatever is worshipped besides Allah…

The QuranSurah Al-Baqara, 2:256

لَآ إِكْرَاهَ فِى ٱلدِّينِ ۖ قَد تَّبَيَّنَ ٱلرُّشْدُ مِنَ ٱلْغَىِّ ۚ فَمَن يَكْفُرْ بِٱلطَّٰغُوتِ وَيُؤْمِنۢ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدِ ٱسْتَمْسَكَ بِٱلْعُرْوَةِ ٱلْوُثْقَىٰ لَا ٱنفِصَامَ لَهَا ۗ وَٱللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ ﴿٢٥٦﴾

There shall be no compulsion in [acceptance of] the religion. The right course has become clear from the wrong. So whoever disbelieves in Taghut and believes in Allah has grasped the most trustworthy handhold with no break in it. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing. (256)

Translation: Saheeh International

3. The declaration of Ibrahim (peace be upon him): a living picture of the kalimah

Ibrahim (peace be upon him) said to his father and his people: «إِنَّنِى بَرَآءٌ مِّمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ»: I am disassociated from what you worship (negation), «إِلَّا ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَنِى»: except the One who created me (affirmation). Then Allah said that he made it a word remaining among his descendants. In Surat al-Mumtahanah, Ibrahim and those with him are declared an excellent example because of this very disassociation.

The QuranSurah Az-Zukhruf, 43:26-28

وَإِذْ قَالَ إِبْرَٰهِيمُ لِأَبِيهِ وَقَوْمِهِۦٓ إِنَّنِى بَرَآءٌۭ مِّمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ ﴿٢٦﴾ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَنِى فَإِنَّهُۥ سَيَهْدِينِ ﴿٢٧﴾ وَجَعَلَهَا كَلِمَةًۢ بَاقِيَةًۭ فِى عَقِبِهِۦ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْجِعُونَ ﴿٢٨﴾

And [mention, O Muhammad], when Abraham said to his father and his people, "Indeed, I am disassociated from that which you worship (26) Except for He who created me; and indeed, He will guide me." (27) And he made it a word remaining among his descendants that they might return [to it]. (28)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Mumtahana, 60:4

قَدْ كَانَتْ لَكُمْ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌۭ فِىٓ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ وَٱلَّذِينَ مَعَهُۥٓ إِذْ قَالُوا۟ لِقَوْمِهِمْ إِنَّا بُرَءَٰٓؤُا۟ مِنكُمْ وَمِمَّا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَفَرْنَا بِكُمْ وَبَدَا بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمُ ٱلْعَدَٰوَةُ وَٱلْبَغْضَآءُ أَبَدًا حَتَّىٰ تُؤْمِنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَحْدَهُۥٓ إِلَّا قَوْلَ إِبْرَٰهِيمَ لِأَبِيهِ لَأَسْتَغْفِرَنَّ لَكَ وَمَآ أَمْلِكُ لَكَ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَىْءٍۢ ۖ رَّبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ ٱلْمَصِيرُ ﴿٤﴾

There has already been for you an excellent pattern in Abraham and those with him, when they said to their people, "Indeed, we are disassociated from you and from whatever you worship other than Allah. We have denied you, and there has appeared between us and you animosity and hatred forever until you believe in Allah alone" except for the saying of Abraham to his father, "I will surely ask forgiveness for you, but I have not [power to do] for you anything against Allah. Our Lord, upon You we have relied, and to You we have returned, and to You is the destination. (4)

Translation: Saheeh International

4. Allah alone is the Truth; all else is falsehood

In Surat al-Hajj He said: that is because Allah is the Truth and what they call upon besides Him is falsehood. In Surat al-Anbiya' He gave the proof that if there had been gods besides Allah in the heavens and the earth, both would have been ruined. And in Surat an-Nahl the command is explicit: do not take two gods; He is only one God.

The QuranSurah Al-Hajj, 22:62

ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِۦ هُوَ ٱلْبَٰطِلُ وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْعَلِىُّ ٱلْكَبِيرُ ﴿٦٢﴾

That is because Allah is the Truth, and that which they call upon other than Him is falsehood, and because Allah is the Most High, the Grand. (62)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Anbiyaa, 21:22

لَوْ كَانَ فِيهِمَآ ءَالِهَةٌ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ لَفَسَدَتَا ۚ فَسُبْحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَرْشِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ ﴿٢٢﴾

Had there been within the heavens and earth gods besides Allah, they both would have been ruined. So exalted is Allah, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe. (22)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah An-Nahl, 16:51

۞ وَقَالَ ٱللَّهُ لَا تَتَّخِذُوٓا۟ إِلَٰهَيْنِ ٱثْنَيْنِ ۖ إِنَّمَا هُوَ إِلَٰهٌۭ وَٰحِدٌۭ ۖ فَإِيَّٰىَ فَٱرْهَبُونِ ﴿٥١﴾

And Allah has said, "Do not take for yourselves two deities. He is but one God, so fear only Me." (51)

Translation: Saheeh International

5. The requirements of the kalimah: in the light of the texts

The qualities of the one who says the kalimah, as described in the Quran and the two Sahihs, make its requirements clear:

Knowledge: «فَٱعْلَمْ» (Muhammad 47:19) and «مَنْ مَاتَ وَهُوَ يَعْلَمُ» (Sahih Muslim 26). Certainty: believing and then not doubting (al-Hujurat 49:15) and «غَيْرَ شَاكٍّ» (Sahih Muslim 27). Sincerity: making the religion purely for Allah (az-Zumar 39:2-3) and «خَالِصًا مِنْ قَلْبِهِ» (Sahih al-Bukhari 99). Truthfulness: «صِدْقًا مِنْ قَلْبِهِ» (Sahih al-Bukhari 128). Love: the believers are stronger in love for Allah than anything else (al-Baqarah 2:165). Rejecting worship of other than Allah: (al-Baqarah 2:256; Sahih Muslim 23). Acceptance and absence of arrogance: being arrogant on hearing the kalimah is described as a trait of the deniers (as-Saffat 37:35).

The QuranSurah Al-Hujuraat, 49:15

إِنَّمَا ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ بِٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ لَمْ يَرْتَابُوا۟ وَجَٰهَدُوا۟ بِأَمْوَٰلِهِمْ وَأَنفُسِهِمْ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ أُو۟لَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلصَّٰدِقُونَ ﴿١٥﴾

The believers are only the ones who have believed in Allah and His Messenger and then doubt not but strive with their properties and their lives in the cause of Allah. It is those who are the truthful. (15)

Translation: Saheeh International

The bondsman who would meet Allah without entertaining any doubt about these (two fundamentals) would enter heaven.

And those who take protectors besides Him [say], "We only worship them that they may bring us nearer to Allah in position." Indeed, Allah will judge between them concerning that over which they differ. Indeed, Allah does not guide he who is a liar and [confirmed] disbeliever. (3)

Translation: Saheeh International

6. The status and virtue of the kalimah

Islam is built upon five things and the first of them is testifying to this kalimah. Of the seventy-odd branches of faith, the highest is saying «La ilaha illallah». Whoever dies without having associated anything with Allah will enter Paradise; and whoever said this kalimah with an atom's weight of good in his heart will also eventually be brought out of Hell. Also, a great virtue is mentioned for saying «La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah…» one hundred times.

Islam is built upon five

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 8

«بُنِيَ الإِسْلاَمُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ شَهَادَةِ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلاَةِ، وَإِيتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، وَالْحَجِّ، وَصَوْمِ رَمَضَانَ»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated Ibn 'Umar: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said: Islam is based on (the following) five (principles): 1. To testify that none has the right to be worshipped but Allah and Muhammad is Allah's Messenger (ﷺ). 2. To offer the (compulsory congregational) prayers dutifully and perfectly. 3. To pay Zakat (i.e. obligatory charity) . 4. To perform Hajj. (i.e. Pilgrimage to Mecca) 5. To observe fast during the month of Ramadan.

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

Narrated Abu Huraira: Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said," Whoever says: "La ilaha illal-lah wahdahu la sharika lahu, lahu-l-mulk wa lahul- hamd wa huwa 'ala kulli shai'in qadir," one hundred times will get the same reward as given for manumitting ten slaves; and one hundred good deeds will be written in his accounts, and one hundred sins will be deducted from his accounts, and it (his saying) will be a shield for him from Satan on that day till night, and nobody will be able to do a better deed except the one who does more than he."

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ ، عَنْ سُمَىٍّ ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏"‏ مَنْ قَالَ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ، وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهْوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ‏.‏ فِي يَوْمٍ مِائَةَ مَرَّةٍ، كَانَتْ لَهُ عَدْلَ عَشْرِ رِقَابٍ، وَكُتِبَ لَهُ مِائَةُ حَسَنَةٍ، وَمُحِيَتْ عَنْهُ مِائَةُ سَيِّئَةٍ، وَكَانَتْ لَهُ حِرْزًا مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ يَوْمَهُ ذَلِكَ، حَتَّى يُمْسِيَ، وَلَمْ يَأْتِ أَحَدٌ بِأَفْضَلَ مِمَّا جَاءَ بِهِ إِلاَّ رَجُلٌ عَمِلَ أَكْثَرَ مِنْهُ ‏" ‏‏.‏

Summary

  • Meaning: there is no god (worthy of worship) except Allah (Muhammad 47:19; Al 'Imran 3:18).
  • Negation «La ilaha» + affirmation «illallah» = rejecting taghut and believing in Allah (al-Baqarah 2:256).
  • Rejecting worship of other than Allah is a necessary part of the kalimah (Sahih Muslim 23).
  • Ibrahim's declaration of disassociation is a living picture of the kalimah (az-Zukhruf 43:26-28).
  • Requirements: knowledge, certainty, sincerity, truthfulness, love, rejecting all else, acceptance.
  • The first pillar of Islam and the highest branch of faith (Sahih al-Bukhari 8; Sahih Muslim 35).
