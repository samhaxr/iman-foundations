Quiz for this lesson

In the Hadith of Jibril, the first part of the definition of faith is «أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاللَّهِ» (Sahih Muslim 8; Sahih al-Bukhari 50). The Quran describes belief in Allah from several angles: the proof of His existence, His being the Lord of everything (Creator, Owner, Disposer of affairs), His being the only one worthy of worship, and His beautiful names and lofty attributes. In this lesson we will look at each aspect through the texts.

1. The existence of Allah

The Quran addresses the intellect and raises the question: were they created by nothing, or are they themselves the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? The messengers said to their peoples: is there any doubt about Allah, Creator of the heavens and the earth?

The QuranSurah At-Tur, 52:35-36

أَمْ خُلِقُوا۟ مِنْ غَيْرِ شَىْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ ٱلْخَٰلِقُونَ ﴿٣٥﴾ أَمْ خَلَقُوا۟ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ ۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ ﴿٣٦﴾

Or were they created by nothing, or were they the creators [of themselves]? (35) Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain. (36)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Ibrahim, 14:10

۞ قَالَتْ رُسُلُهُمْ أَفِى ٱللَّهِ شَكٌّۭ فَاطِرِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۖ يَدْعُوكُمْ لِيَغْفِرَ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمْ وَيُؤَخِّرَكُمْ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍۢ مُّسَمًّۭى ۚ قَالُوٓا۟ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُنَا تُرِيدُونَ أَن تَصُدُّونَا عَمَّا كَانَ يَعْبُدُ ءَابَآؤُنَا فَأْتُونَا بِسُلْطَٰنٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ ﴿١٠﴾

Their messengers said, "Can there be doubt about Allah, Creator of the heavens and earth? He invites you that He may forgive you of your sins, and He delays your death for a specified term." They said, "You are not but men like us who wish to avert us from what our fathers were worshipping. So bring us a clear authority." (10)

Translation: Saheeh International

2. Tawhid of lordship: Allah alone is Creator, Owner and Disposer

In the second verse of Surat al-Fatihah Allah is called "Lord of the worlds". He alone is Creator of the heavens and the earth; He covers the night with the day, and the sun, moon and stars are subjected by His command: «أَلَا لَهُ ٱلْخَلْقُ وَٱلْأَمْرُ». He is Creator of all things and Guardian over them, and there is no creator other than Him who provides from the heaven and the earth. In the supplication of the Prophet ﷺ: O Lord of the heavens, Lord of the earth, Lord of the Mighty Throne, our Lord and Lord of everything. And on the Day of Resurrection Allah will grasp the earth and say: I am the King; where are the kings of the earth?

The QuranSurah Al-Faatiha, 1:2

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ﴿٢﴾

[All] praise is [due] to Allah, Lord of the worlds - (2)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-A'raaf, 7:54

إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍۢ ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ يُغْشِى ٱلَّيْلَ ٱلنَّهَارَ يَطْلُبُهُۥ حَثِيثًۭا وَٱلشَّمْسَ وَٱلْقَمَرَ وَٱلنُّجُومَ مُسَخَّرَٰتٍۭ بِأَمْرِهِۦٓ ۗ أَلَا لَهُ ٱلْخَلْقُ وَٱلْأَمْرُ ۗ تَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ﴿٥٤﴾

Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and earth in six days and then established Himself above the Throne. He covers the night with the day, [another night] chasing it rapidly; and [He created] the sun, the moon, and the stars, subjected by His command. Unquestionably, His is the creation and the command; blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds. (54)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Az-Zumar, 39:62

ٱللَّهُ خَٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ ۖ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ وَكِيلٌۭ ﴿٦٢﴾

Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is, over all things, Disposer of affairs. (62)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Faatir, 35:3

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱذْكُرُوا۟ نِعْمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ ۚ هَلْ مِنْ خَٰلِقٍ غَيْرُ ٱللَّهِ يَرْزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ فَأَنَّىٰ تُؤْفَكُونَ ﴿٣﴾

O mankind, remember the favor of Allah upon you. Is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the heaven and earth? There is no deity except Him, so how are you deluded? (3)

Translation: Saheeh International

You are the First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

3. Acknowledging lordship requires worship of Him alone

The Quran tells us that when the polytheists were asked who provides for you, who owns hearing and sight, who brings the living out of the dead and who arranges every matter, they would say: Allah! Likewise, to whom does the earth belong, who is Lord of the seven heavens and the Mighty Throne, in whose hand is the dominion of everything, answer: Allah! Yet each time the Quran asked: then will you not remember? Will you not fear Him? That is, merely accepting that Allah is the Creator is not enough; the necessary consequence of this acknowledgement is that worship, too, be for Him alone.

The QuranSurah Yunus, 10:31

قُلْ مَن يَرْزُقُكُم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ أَمَّن يَمْلِكُ ٱلسَّمْعَ وَٱلْأَبْصَٰرَ وَمَن يُخْرِجُ ٱلْحَىَّ مِنَ ٱلْمَيِّتِ وَيُخْرِجُ ٱلْمَيِّتَ مِنَ ٱلْحَىِّ وَمَن يُدَبِّرُ ٱلْأَمْرَ ۚ فَسَيَقُولُونَ ٱللَّهُ ۚ فَقُلْ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ ﴿٣١﴾

Say, "Who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? Or who controls hearing and sight and who brings the living out of the dead and brings the dead out of the living and who arranges [every] matter?" They will say, "Allah," so say, "Then will you not fear Him?" (31)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Muminoon, 23:84-89

قُل لِّمَنِ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَمَن فِيهَآ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٨٤﴾ سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِ ۚ قُلْ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ ﴿٨٥﴾ قُلْ مَن رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلسَّبْعِ وَرَبُّ ٱلْعَرْشِ ٱلْعَظِيمِ ﴿٨٦﴾ سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِ ۚ قُلْ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ ﴿٨٧﴾ قُلْ مَنۢ بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ وَهُوَ يُجِيرُ وَلَا يُجَارُ عَلَيْهِ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٨٨﴾ سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِ ۚ قُلْ فَأَنَّىٰ تُسْحَرُونَ ﴿٨٩﴾

Say, [O Muhammad], "To whom belongs the earth and whoever is in it, if you should know?" (84) They will say, "To Allah." Say, "Then will you not remember?" (85) Say, "Who is Lord of the seven heavens and Lord of the Great Throne?" (86) They will say, "[They belong] to Allah." Say, "Then will you not fear Him?" (87) Say, "In whose hand is the realm of all things - and He protects while none can protect against Him - if you should know?" (88) They will say, "[All belongs] to Allah." Say, "Then how are you deluded?" (89)

Translation: Saheeh International

4. Tawhid of worship: worship belongs to Allah alone

In Surat al-Baqarah, after listing signs of lordship (He made the earth a bed and the sky a canopy, and sent down rain to bring forth fruits), the command came: so do not knowingly set up rivals to Allah. Your god is one God; there is no god but Him. To Musa (peace be upon him) He said: Indeed I am Allah; there is no god but Me, so worship Me. And the Prophet ﷺ was asked which sin is greatest. He said: that you set up a rival to Allah while He created you.

The QuranSurah Al-Baqara, 2:21-22

يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُمْ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ ﴿٢١﴾ ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فِرَٰشًۭا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءًۭ وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءًۭ فَأَخْرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزْقًۭا لَّكُمْ ۖ فَلَا تَجْعَلُوا۟ لِلَّهِ أَندَادًۭا وَأَنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ﴿٢٢﴾

O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, that you may become righteous - (21) [He] who made for you the earth a bed [spread out] and the sky a ceiling and sent down from the sky, rain and brought forth thereby fruits as provision for you. So do not attribute to Allah equals while you know [that there is nothing similar to Him]. (22)

Translation: Saheeh International

The QuranSurah Al-Baqara, 2:163

And your god is one God. There is no deity [worthy of worship] except Him, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. (163)

Translation: Saheeh International

Translation: Saheeh International

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 4477

5. Names and attributes: beautiful names and lofty attributes

«لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِۦ شَىْءٌ ۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْبَصِيرُ»: there is nothing like Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing. This one verse contains both matters: negation of any resemblance to creation, and affirmation of Allah's attributes (hearing and seeing). Allah has beautiful names; we are commanded to call on Him by them and to leave those who deviate concerning His names. The closing verses of Surat al-Hashr, Surat al-Ikhlas and Ayat al-Kursi are comprehensive statements of Allah's names and attributes.

The QuranSurah Ash-Shura, 42:11

فَاطِرُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَٰجًۭا وَمِنَ ٱلْأَنْعَٰمِ أَزْوَٰجًۭا ۖ يَذْرَؤُكُمْ فِيهِ ۚ لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِۦ شَىْءٌۭ ۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْبَصِيرُ ﴿١١﴾

[He is] Creator of the heavens and the earth. He has made for you from yourselves, mates, and among the cattle, mates; He multiplies you thereby. There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing. (11)

6. Names and attributes in the two Sahihs

The Prophet ﷺ told us that Allah has ninety-nine names; whoever preserves them (ahsaha) will enter Paradise. A Companion would end his recitation in every rak'ah with «Qul huwa Allahu ahad», because it is the description of ar-Rahman; the Prophet ﷺ said: tell him that Allah loves him. Allah does not sleep, and it does not befit Him to sleep. We are not calling upon one who is deaf or absent, but upon One who hears and sees. Every night in the last third He descends to the lowest heaven and calls: who is supplicating to Me so that I may answer him? He is the First, the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden.

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

7. The Most Merciful Lord

When Allah created the creation, He wrote in His Book (which is with Him above the Throne): My mercy prevails over My anger. He made mercy in one hundred parts, kept ninety-nine with Himself and sent down one part to the earth; it is through that one part that creatures show mercy to one another. Seeing a mother clasp her child to her chest, the Prophet ﷺ said: Allah is far more merciful to His servants than she is to her child. And Allah says: I am as My servant thinks of Me, and I am with him when he remembers Me.

حَدَّثَنَا الْحَكَمُ بْنُ نَافِعٍ ، أَخْبَرَنَا شُعَيْبٌ ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ ، أَخْبَرَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ الْمُسَيَّبِ ، أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَقُولُ ‏"‏ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ الرَّحْمَةَ مِائَةَ جُزْءٍ، فَأَمْسَكَ عِنْدَهُ تِسْعَةً وَتِسْعِينَ جُزْءًا، وَأَنْزَلَ فِي الأَرْضِ جُزْءًا وَاحِدًا، فَمِنْ ذَلِكَ الْجُزْءِ يَتَرَاحَمُ الْخَلْقُ، حَتَّى تَرْفَعَ الْفَرَسُ حَافِرَهَا عَنْ وَلَدِهَا خَشْيَةَ أَنْ تُصِيبَهُ ‏" ‏‏.‏

More merciful to His servants than a mother

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 5999

«لَلَّهُ أَرْحَمُ بِعِبَادِهِ مِنْ هَذِهِ بِوَلَدِهَا»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated `Umar bin Al-Khattab: Some Sabi (i.e. war prisoners, children and woman only) were brought before the Prophet (ﷺ) and behold, a woman amongst them was milking her breasts to feed and whenever she found a child amongst the captives, she took it over her chest and nursed it (she had lost her child but later she found him) the Prophet said to us, "Do you think that this lady can throw her son in the fire?" We replied, "No, if she has the power not to throw it (in the fire)." The Prophet (ﷺ) then said, "Allah is more merciful to His slaves than this lady to her son."

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ أَبِي مَرْيَمَ ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو غَسَّانَ ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي زَيْدُ بْنُ أَسْلَمَ ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَدِمَ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم سَبْىٌ، فَإِذَا امْرَأَةٌ مِنَ السَّبْىِ قَدْ تَحْلُبُ ثَدْيَهَا تَسْقِي، إِذَا وَجَدَتْ صَبِيًّا فِي السَّبْىِ أَخَذَتْهُ فَأَلْصَقَتْهُ بِبَطْنِهَا وَأَرْضَعَتْهُ، فَقَالَ لَنَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ أَتَرَوْنَ هَذِهِ طَارِحَةً وَلَدَهَا فِي النَّارِ ‏"‏‏.‏ قُلْنَا لاَ وَهْىَ تَقْدِرُ عَلَى أَنْ لاَ تَطْرَحَهُ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏"‏ ا للَّهُ أَرْحَمُ بِعِبَادِهِ مِنْ هَذِهِ بِوَلَدِهَا ‏" ‏‏.‏

I am as My servant thinks of Me

Sahih al-Bukhari Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 7405

«أَنَا عِنْدَ ظَنِّ عَبْدِي بِي، وَأَنَا مَعَهُ إِذَا ذَكَرَنِي»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

Narrated Abu Huraira: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Allah says: 'I am just as My slave thinks I am, (i.e. I am able to do for him what he thinks I can do for him) and I am with him if He remembers Me. If he remembers Me in himself, I too, remember him in Myself; and if he remembers Me in a group of people, I remember him in a group that is better than they; and if he comes one span nearer to Me, I go one cubit nearer to him; and if he comes one cubit nearer to Me, I go a distance of two outstretched arms nearer to him; and if he comes to Me walking, I go to him running.' "

Full Arabic text (with isnad), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا عُمَرُ بْنُ حَفْصٍ ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي ، حَدَّثَنَا الأَعْمَشُ ، سَمِعْتُ أَبَا صَالِحٍ ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ يَقُولُ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى أَنَا عِنْدَ ظَنِّ عَبْدِي بِي، وَأَنَا مَعَهُ إِذَا ذَكَرَنِي، فَإِنْ ذَكَرَنِي فِي نَفْسِهِ ذَكَرْتُهُ فِي نَفْسِي، وَإِنْ ذَكَرَنِي فِي مَلأٍ ذَكَرْتُهُ فِي مَلأٍ خَيْرٍ مِنْهُمْ، وَإِنْ تَقَرَّبَ إِلَىَّ بِشِبْرٍ تَقَرَّبْتُ إِلَيْهِ ذِرَاعًا، وَإِنْ تَقَرَّبَ إِلَىَّ ذِرَاعًا تَقَرَّبْتُ إِلَيْهِ بَاعًا، وَإِنْ أَتَانِي يَمْشِي أَتَيْتُهُ هَرْوَلَةً ‏" ‏‏.‏

Summary

  • Existence: nothing came into being without a Creator (at-Tur 52:35-36).
  • Lordship: Allah alone is Lord of the worlds, Creator and Disposer (al-Fatihah 1:2; al-A'raf 7:54; az-Zumar 39:62).
  • Acknowledging lordship requires worship (Yunus 10:31; al-Mu'minun 23:84-89).
  • Worship: worship belongs to Allah alone; the greatest sin is shirk (al-Baqarah 2:21-22; Sahih al-Bukhari 4477).
  • Names and attributes: there is nothing like Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing (ash-Shura 42:11).
  • Allah's ninety-nine names; His mercy prevails over His anger (Sahih al-Bukhari 2736, 3194).
